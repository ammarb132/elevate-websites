What We Do

Electrical services for every part of your property

From a single flickering outlet to a full panel replacement, our crews handle it under permit and to code.

Panel Upgrades

Replace outdated or overloaded panels with a right-sized, code-compliant service to power your home safely.

EV Charger Installation

Level 2 charger circuits sized correctly and installed to manufacturer spec for fast, safe home charging.

Electrical Repairs

Flickering lights, dead outlets, tripping breakers, we diagnose the root cause and fix it, not just the symptom.

Lighting Design & Installation

Recessed, accent, and landscape lighting planned and wired to complement how each room is actually used.

Whole-Home Rewiring

Replace aging or unsafe wiring throughout the house with minimal disruption to walls and finishes.

Generator Installation

Automatic standby generators sized for your home, wired with a transfer switch for seamless backup power.

Ceiling Fan & Fixture Installation

Proper box, bracing, and circuit support so fans and fixtures run quiet, level, and safe for years.

Electrical Safety Inspections

A full-property inspection with a written report, ideal before buying, selling, or after an older-home purchase.
