The parlor · our story

The gathering place,
in the heart of downtown.

A milking parlor is the central gathering place on a dairy farm where cows are relieved of their burdens. At The Milk Parlor in Blacksburg, we will help relieve your burdens as well.

An elaborate grilled cheese menu, White Russians worth the trip, a Virginia craft tap list, and live entertainment to close the night. All in the heart of downtown.

“Milking good nights.”That’s the whole place in three words.
